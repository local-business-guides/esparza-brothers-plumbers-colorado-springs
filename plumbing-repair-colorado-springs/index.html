A simple repair-preparation checklist

Before you call, take a moment to gather the details that help explain the situation. Note the room or area, the item involved, when the symptoms began, and whether the issue is constant or intermittent. If there is visible water, say where it is appearing. Mention any change you have noticed, without assuming what caused it.

Keep these points handy:
- What is affected, such as a fixture or plumbing area.
- When you first noticed the issue.
- Whether it has happened before or is still occurring.
- Any steps you have already taken.
- Questions about assessment, repair, and what to expect next.
